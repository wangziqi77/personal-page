    Why the answer changes

    • Stable, large-volume basics make a low factory quote more valuable.
    • Rapid changes raise the cost of long lead times and unsold or missing stock.
    • Specialised fabric and production clusters can outweigh cheaper sewing.
    • Compliance depends on this supplier's evidence and material traceability, not a country label.

    What the game compares

    Factory price · tariff exposure · transport · lead time · inventory risk · compliance risk. Lead time is quoted delivery speed; inventory risk reflects demand uncertainty and repeat-order flexibility. Lower weighted score wins.
